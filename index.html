I work as a Security Researcher for Cisco Talos, where I explore the connections between cybersecurity, AI, and emerging technology policy. Before joining Cisco Talos, I spent time as a Cybersecurity Researcher at Splunk SURGe, digging into threat intelligence and the evolving techniques that define today’s security landscape. I hold a Master’s degree in Anticipatory Intelligence, a field focused on forecasting risk and navigating complex, uncertain futures. Below is a showcase of some of my work ranging from technical projects to geopolitical research.
